Wapari Privacy Policy

Grain and commodity trading account management app

Effective date: 5 November 2026
App: Wapari
Privacy contact: awasikharal@gmail.com

Wapari is designed to keep your trading account records on your device. Online features such as optional Google Drive backup and advertising involve the relevant third-party services described below.

1. Information stored on your device

Account names, commodity/trade details, quantities, prices, notes, app preferences, and backup/merge records that you enter are stored locally on your device using the app's local storage. Wapari does not operate its own account-registration system or its own application server for these records. Anyone with access to your unlocked device or exported backup may be able to see the information in it. Please protect your device and backup files.

2. Optional Google Drive backup

If you choose Google Drive Backup or Restore, Wapari uses Google Sign-In and requests the Google Drive drive.appdata scope. The backup file is stored in the app-specific hidden application data area of your Google Drive account and can be retrieved by Wapari when you choose Restore. The backup may contain the account and trading information you entered in Wapari. Google processes sign-in and Drive data under its own privacy terms. Google Drive backup is optional; you can continue using the app without it.

To remove a cloud backup, manage the Wapari app data or revoke the app's access from your Google Account settings. Removing the app from your device alone may not remove a backup already stored in Google Drive.

3. Nearby device transfer, QR and sharing

If you choose the mobile-to-mobile transfer feature, Wapari uses nearby-device connectivity and the permissions required by Android to discover/connect to a nearby device and transfer the backup you select. Transfer content is sent to the receiving device you choose. Do not accept a transfer from a device you do not trust. If you choose to share or send information through another app (for example, SMS or WhatsApp), that app and its provider handle the information under their own terms.

4. Advertising

Wapari displays a banner advertisement using Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Google and its advertising partners may process device identifiers, IP address, app interaction and diagnostic information, and other data as described in Google's policies, for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and, where permitted and configured, personalization. You can review Google's advertising and privacy information at Google Privacy Policy and AdMob and publisher policies. Advertising choices and consent requirements may vary by region and device settings.

5. Camera, QR scanning, documents and printing

When you use QR scanning, the app requests camera access to read the code. The scan is used to perform the feature you selected. Wapari's PDF/report feature generates documents from information in your records. If you print or share a document using Android's print/share services, the selected system service or third-party app may process that document.

6. Data sharing and service providers

Wapari does not sell your locally stored trading records. Information is shared outside the app only when you initiate a feature that requires it, such as Google Drive backup/restore, nearby transfer, or sharing/printing through another app, and through the AdMob advertising SDK described above. Google services and other selected apps operate under their own privacy policies.

7. Data retention and deletion

Local records remain on your device until you delete them within the app, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app (Android behavior and backups may vary). Exported backup copies remain wherever you save or share them. Google Drive backups remain in your Google account until you delete the app data/backup or revoke/manage access as described above. Wapari does not have a central Wapari user account or central account database to delete. You are responsible for deleting copies that you exported or sent to another service.

8. Security

Wapari uses Android's app sandbox and the security mechanisms provided by the operating system and its integrated services. No storage or transmission method is completely risk-free. Keep Android updated, use a secure device lock, and avoid sharing backups with untrusted people.

9. Children's privacy

Wapari is a general-purpose trading and account-management tool and is not designed specifically for children. It does not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when app features, service providers, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published at this webpage with its effective date.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the Wapari developer at awasikharal@gmail.com.
